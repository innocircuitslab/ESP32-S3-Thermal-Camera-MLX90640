# ESP32 Thermal Camera Enclosure V4 REAL-PRINT FIX — Print Guide

## Print these first

Before a full enclosure print, print the two corner mating coupons:

- `05_corner_body_fit_test.stl`
- `06_corner_cover_fit_test.stl`

They are cropped directly from the real V4 body and cover, so they test the exact screw-boss / locating-lip geometry.

The cover should drop fully onto the corner with no gap.

## Full enclosure parts

- `01_front_body_V4_REAL_PRINT_FIX.stl`
- `02_rear_cover_V4_REAL_PRINT_FIX.stl`

## Suggested print settings

- PLA or PETG
- 0.20 mm layer height
- 0.4 mm nozzle
- 4 walls/perimeters
- 15–25% infill
- Front body: display/front face on the build plate
- Rear cover: outside/rear face on the build plate

## Fasteners

4 × M2.5 self-tapping screws. Start with roughly 10 mm length because the boss tops are deliberately recessed.

## V3 cover clearance

- Locating-lip depth: 2.0 mm
- Screw-boss recess below rim: 3.5 mm
- Nominal vertical clearance: 1.5 mm
- Lip relief around each boss: Ø10.5 mm
