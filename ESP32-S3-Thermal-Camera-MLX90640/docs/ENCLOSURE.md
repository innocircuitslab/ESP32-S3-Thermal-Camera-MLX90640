# 3D-printed enclosure — V4 REAL-PRINT FIX

The repository includes the V4 enclosure files produced after testing the first full print.

## Main dimensions

- Front body: **90 × 62 × 32 mm**
- Usable internal depth to locating lip: **27.4 mm**
- Display opening: **55 × 36 mm**
- USB service slot: **18 × 9 mm**, centered on the side
- Rear-cover lip penetration: **2.0 mm**
- Screw-boss recess below body rim: **3.5 mm**
- Nominal boss-to-lip vertical clearance: **1.5 mm**
- Rear lip XY clearance: **0.6 mm per side**
- Fasteners: **4 × M2.5 self-tapping screws**

## What changed in V4

V4 incorporates the first real-print feedback:

1. The enclosure body was made deeper so the electronics and wiring fit without preventing the rear cover from closing.
2. The display opening was enlarged.
3. The USB service slot was centered and widened for easier cable movement.
4. Screw posts were rebuilt so they start directly from the enclosure floor rather than beginning above it.
5. Boss/lip clearances were preserved so the rear cover can seat fully.

## Suggested print settings

- PLA or PETG
- 0.20 mm layer height
- 0.4 mm nozzle
- 4 walls/perimeters
- 15–25% infill
- Front body: front/display face on the build plate
- Rear cover: outside/rear face on the build plate

The original V4 STEP files, STL files, fit-test coupons, source generator and validation report are all included under `enclosure/V4_REAL_PRINT_FIX/`.
